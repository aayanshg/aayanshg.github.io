## Chocolate cookies without egg {#chocolate-cookies-without-egg}

### Ingredients

- Softened unsalted butter: ½ cup (about 110 g)
- Granulated white sugar: ½ cup (100 g)
- Light brown sugar: ½ cup (100 g)
- Milk, at room temperature: 3 tablespoons, plus 1 tablespoon extra if needed
- Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon
- All-purpose flour: 1 cup (125 g)
- Unsweetened cocoa powder: ⅔ cup (about 65 g)
- Baking soda: 1 teaspoon
- Salt: ¼ teaspoon
- Chocolate chunks or chips: 1½ cups total

### Method

1. Beat the softened butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together until light and creamy.
2. Stir in the vanilla and 3 tablespoons of milk.
3. In a separate bowl, sift and whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
4. Fold the dry mixture into the butter mixture just until a thick dough forms. Add the extra tablespoon of milk if the dough feels too dry.
5. Fold in the chocolate chunks or chips.
6. Roll the dough into balls and chill for 30–60 minutes so the cookies do not spread too thin.
7. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Bake the dough balls on a parchment-lined tray for 10–12 minutes.
8. Let the cookies rest on the hot tray for a few minutes, then move them to a wire rack.

## Chocolate cookies {#chocolate-cookies}

### Ingredients

- Butter: ¾ cup (170 g), softened
- Granulated sugar: 1½ cups
- Large eggs: 2
- Vanilla extract: 2 teaspoons
- All-purpose flour: 2 cups (260 g)
- Unsweetened cocoa powder: ⅔ cup (60 g)
- Baking soda: 1 teaspoon
- Salt: ½ teaspoon
- Coarse sugar or extra chocolate chips for topping (optional)

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Cream the softened butter and sugar until smooth and fluffy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
4. Gradually add the dry mixture to the butter mixture, mixing just until combined.
5. Scoop rounded portions of dough onto the baking sheet, leaving about 2 inches between cookies. Add coarse sugar or chocolate chips on top if desired.
6. Bake for 8–10 minutes, until the edges are set and the centers are still soft.
7. Rest the cookies on the pan for a few minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool.

## Coconut cookies without egg {#coconut-cookies-without-egg}

### Ingredients

- All-purpose flour (maida): 1 cup (130–150 g)
- Unsalted butter: ½ cup (113 g), softened
- Powdered or granulated sugar: ½–⅔ cup (100–135 g)
- Unsweetened desiccated coconut: 1 cup total, divided
- Baking powder: ½ teaspoon
- Salt: ¼ teaspoon
- Vanilla essence: ½ teaspoon (optional)
- Milk: 1–2 tablespoons, only if needed to bind the dough

### Method

1. Beat the softened butter and sugar for 3–5 minutes, until pale, light, and creamy. Mix in the vanilla if using.
2. Sift in the flour, baking powder, and salt. Add ½ cup of the desiccated coconut.
3. Fold the mixture together with a spatula or your hands. If it is too dry to hold its shape, add 1–2 tablespoons of milk and gently knead into a soft dough. Do not overmix.
4. Wrap the dough and refrigerate for 30 minutes.
5. Heat the oven to 170°C (338°F) and line a baking tray with parchment paper.
6. Roll small portions into balls and gently flatten them into discs. Press or roll the tops in the remaining ½ cup of coconut.
7. Place the discs slightly apart and bake for 15–17 minutes, until the edges turn light golden brown.
8. Cool completely on a wire rack so the cookies firm up.

## Coconut cookies {#coconut-cookies}

### Ingredients

- All-purpose flour: 1¼ cups
- Baking soda: ½ teaspoon
- Unsalted butter: ½ cup, softened
- Packed brown sugar: ½ cup
- Granulated white sugar: ½ cup
- Large egg, at room temperature: 1
- Vanilla extract: ½ teaspoon
- Shredded or flaked coconut: 1⅓–1½ cups
- Salt: a pinch

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.
3. Beat the butter, brown sugar, and white sugar for about 3 minutes, until smooth and fluffy.
4. Beat in the egg and vanilla until well combined.
5. Gradually stir in the flour mixture just until a dough forms. Fold in the coconut without overmixing.
6. Scoop rounded tablespoons of dough onto the tray, leaving 2–3 inches between cookies.
7. Bake for 8–12 minutes, until the edges turn light golden brown.
8. Rest the cookies on the tray for a couple of minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

## Pancakes {#pancakes}

### Ingredients

- All-purpose flour: 1½ cups
- Baking powder: 3½ teaspoons
- White sugar: 1 tablespoon
- Salt: ¼ teaspoon
- Milk: 1¼ cups
- Butter, melted: 3 tablespoons
- Large egg: 1

### Method

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt together in a large bowl.
2. Make a well in the dry ingredients. Add the milk, melted butter, and egg. Mix gently until smooth, leaving a few small lumps for extra fluffiness.
3. Warm a lightly oiled griddle or non-stick skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Pour about ¼ cup of batter onto the hot surface for each pancake. Cook for 2–3 minutes, until bubbles form and the edges look dry.
5. Flip and cook until the other side is golden. Serve warm with butter and maple syrup.

## Cupcakes {#cupcakes}

Makes 12 cupcakes.

### Ingredients

- All-purpose flour: 1 cup (120–130 g)
- Granulated or caster sugar: ½ cup (100–150 g)
- Large eggs, at room temperature: 2
- Baking powder: 1 teaspoon
- Vegetable oil or melted butter: ½ cup (about 60–110 ml)
- Milk, at room temperature: ½ cup
- Vanilla extract or essence: 1–2 teaspoons
- Salt: a small pinch

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners.
2. Beat the eggs and sugar for about 1 minute, until slightly pale and fluffy. Stir in the oil or melted butter, milk, and vanilla.
3. In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
4. Gradually fold the dry mixture into the wet ingredients just until smooth. Do not overmix.
5. Divide the batter among the liners, filling each about two-thirds full.
6. Bake for 15–20 minutes. A toothpick inserted in the center should come out clean.
7. Cool in the tin for 5 minutes, then move to a wire rack. Frost after the cupcakes cool completely.

## No-churn ice cream {#ice-cream}

### Ingredients

- Cold heavy cream: 2 cups (480 ml)
- Sweetened condensed milk: 1 can (14 oz / 390 g)
- Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon

### Method

1. Beat the cold heavy cream with an electric mixer on medium-high to high speed until stiff peaks form, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the vanilla and condensed milk.
3. Gently fold everything together with a spatula to keep the mixture light and airy.
4. Transfer to a loaf pan or airtight container. Cover, pressing plastic wrap against the surface to help prevent ice crystals.
5. Freeze for 4–6 hours or overnight.

## Cold coffee {#cold-coffee}

### Ingredients

- Instant coffee granules: 2 teaspoons
- Sugar: 1–2 teaspoons, or to taste
- Warm or room-temperature water: 2 tablespoons
- Chilled milk: 1 cup (about 200–240 ml)
- Ice cubes

### Method

1. Combine the coffee, sugar, and water in a small bowl, jar, or empty water bottle.
2. Seal and shake for 1–2 minutes, or whisk until the mixture turns light brown and foamy. A hand frother also works.
3. Fill a tall glass with ice and pour in the chilled milk.
4. Spoon or pour the coffee foam over the milk. Stir before drinking.

## Gelato {#gelato}

### Ingredients

- Whole milk: 2 cups
- Heavy cream: ½–¾ cup
- Granulated sugar: ½–⅔ cup
- Large egg yolks: 3–4, or 2 tablespoons cornstarch for a Sicilian-style base
- Flavoring: vanilla bean, cocoa powder, or nut paste such as pistachio or hazelnut

### Method

1. Heat the milk and cream in a saucepan over medium heat until steaming and gently bubbling at the edges. Do not boil.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the egg yolks and sugar until thick, pale, and creamy.
3. Slowly ladle some hot dairy into the yolks while whisking constantly to temper them.
4. Pour the tempered mixture back into the saucepan. Cook over low to medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until it coats the back of a spoon (about 175–185°F).
5. Strain into a clean bowl. Cool to room temperature, cover with plastic wrap touching the surface, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
6. Churn the cold base according to the ice cream maker's instructions, usually 20–30 minutes, until it reaches a soft-serve consistency.
7. Transfer to an airtight container and freeze for 2 hours to firm up. Let it sit at room temperature for 10–15 minutes before scooping.

## Carrot cake {#carrot-cake}

### Ingredients

#### Cake layers

- All-purpose flour: 2 cups (250–300 g)
- Baking soda: 2 teaspoons
- Salt: ½ teaspoon
- Ground cinnamon: 1½–2 teaspoons
- Ground nutmeg: ¼–½ teaspoon
- Granulated white sugar: 1 cup (200 g)
- Packed light or dark brown sugar: 1 cup (220 g)
- Vegetable or canola oil: 1¼ cups (300–320 ml)
- Large eggs, at room temperature: 4
- Vanilla extract: 2 teaspoons
- Freshly grated carrots: 3 cups (about 1 lb)
- Chopped pecans or walnuts: ½–1 cup (optional)

#### Cream cheese frosting

- Cream cheese, softened: 8 oz (227 g)
- Unsalted butter, softened: ½ cup (115 g)
- Powdered sugar: 3–4 cups (350–450 g)
- Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon
- Heavy cream or milk: 1–2 tablespoons, if needed for consistency

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease two 9-inch round cake pans and line their bottoms with parchment paper.
2. Whisk together the flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
3. In a separate large bowl, whisk the granulated sugar, brown sugar, oil, eggs, and vanilla until smooth.
4. Gradually stir the dry mixture into the wet mixture just until combined. Do not overmix.
5. Fold in the grated carrots and nuts, if using.
6. Divide the batter between the pans. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool completely in the pans on a wire rack.
7. For the frosting, beat the cream cheese and butter until smooth and fluffy. Add the vanilla, then gradually beat in the powdered sugar. Add a little cream or milk if needed.
8. Place one cooled cake layer on a serving plate and spread frosting over it. Add the second layer and cover the top and sides with the remaining frosting. Garnish with extra nuts if desired.

## Chocolate cake {#chocolate-cake}

### Ingredients

- White sugar: 2 cups
- All-purpose flour: 1¾ cups
- Unsweetened cocoa powder: ¾ cup
- Baking powder: 1½ teaspoons
- Baking soda: 1½ teaspoons
- Salt: 1 teaspoon
- Large eggs: 2
- Milk: 1 cup
- Vegetable oil: ½ cup
- Vanilla extract: 2 teaspoons
- Boiling water or hot coffee: 1 cup

### Method

1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round cake pans.
2. Whisk together the sugar, flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
3. Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla. Beat on medium speed for about 2 minutes, until smooth.
4. Carefully stir in the boiling water or hot coffee by hand. The batter will be thin; this makes a tender crumb.
5. Divide the batter between the pans. Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
6. Cool in the pans for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.

## Crêpes {#crepes}

### Ingredients

- All-purpose flour: 1 cup (125 g)
- Large eggs, at room temperature: 2
- Whole milk: ¾ cup (180 ml)
- Water: ½ cup (120 ml)
- Unsalted butter, melted: 2 tablespoons, plus extra for the pan
- Granulated sugar: 1 tablespoon (optional, for sweet crêpes)
- Salt: ¼ teaspoon
- Vanilla extract: 1 teaspoon (optional)

### Method

1. Combine the eggs, milk, water, melted butter, sugar, salt, and vanilla in a blender or large bowl.
2. Add the flour and blend or whisk until the batter is completely smooth and pourable.
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30–60 minutes, or up to overnight, so the flour hydrates and the crêpes are less likely to tear.
4. Heat an 8-inch non-stick skillet or crêpe pan over medium heat. Lightly brush it with butter.
5. Pour about 3–4 tablespoons (or a scant ¼ cup) of batter into the center. Immediately lift and tilt the pan in a circle to spread the batter thinly.
6. Cook for 1–2 minutes, until the top is no longer wet and the edges are lightly golden and lifting.
7. Loosen the edges with a spatula, flip, and cook the second side for 30–45 seconds.
8. Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining batter, buttering the pan as needed. Fill or top with sweet or savory ingredients.

## Croissants {#croissants}

The original notes do not include measured ingredient amounts. They describe the dough, butter block, lamination, shaping, proofing, and baking process.

### Method

1. **Make the base dough (détrempe).** Combine all-purpose flour, or a mix of bread and all-purpose flour, with water, milk, sugar, yeast, salt, and a small amount of softened butter. Knead on low speed for 5–7 minutes, until smooth and elastic, without overdeveloping the gluten. Shape into a flat square or rectangle, wrap, and refrigerate for 4 hours to overnight.
2. **Prepare the butter block (beurre de tourage).** Use European-style unsalted butter with at least 82% fat. Pound and roll it between parchment into an even 6×8- to 8×10-inch square or rectangle. Keep it chilled but pliable.
3. **Laminate the dough.** Roll the chilled dough to about twice the size of the butter block. Place the butter in the center and fold the dough over it, sealing it completely. Roll into a long rectangle and fold into thirds like a letter, or use a book fold.
4. **Rest and repeat.** Chill the dough for 30–60 minutes between turns to keep the butter cold. Repeat the rolling and folding process 2–3 times. After the final fold, wrap the dough and refrigerate overnight.
5. **Shape.** Roll the laminated dough to an even thickness of about 3.5–5 mm. Trim the edges and cut long triangles. Make a small notch at the wide base, gently stretch the tip, then roll each triangle from base to tip. Place seam-side down on parchment-lined baking sheets.
6. **Proof.** Let the shaped croissants rise at room temperature, or in a humid turned-off oven with a tray of hot water, for 2–4 hours. They should look puffy, doubled, and jiggly.
7. **Bake.** Brush gently with egg wash made from an egg yolk and a little milk or cream. Avoid letting it drip down the sides. Bake at 180–200°C (350–400°F) for 15–25 minutes, until deep golden brown.
8. Cool on a wire rack for at least 20–60 minutes before serving.
